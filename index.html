    The science of Saturday.

    Dijkstra’s shortest-path algorithm

    Austin is dots and lines

    Every road has minutes on it. It adds them up and takes the fastest way: here, 8 + 7 = 15.

    Weighted sampling without replacement

    A lottery with extra tickets

    Places I love get more tickets, far ones get fewer. Every Saturday is new; the favorites keep winning.

    • Pilates → Coffee✓Coffee after Pilates is allowed.
    • Pilates → Nice dinner✕There is a shower missing from this story.

    State machine (rules.step())

    Rules about being a person

    It remembers if you just ate, just worked out or dressed up, and every next stop has to make sense.

    Bitmask dynamic programming

    Building the day, one stop at a time

    It grows every day one stop at a time and keeps only the best version, so billions of orders take under a second.

    Brute-force backtracking tests

    Checking its homework

    A slow version tries every order. The fast one has to match it exactly, or the tests fail.

    Seeded random number generator

    Same number, same Saturday

    Every Saturday has a number. Type it back in and you get the same day, stop for stop.

    Why Austin is the best university town.

    Editor’s letter · By Suhani Tiwari

    What are we doing?

    Dear reader,

    College is expensive. And brunch is never just brunch. It’s the Uber there, the latte you didn’t need, and the “oh, let’s check out that cute shop next door.” Before you know it, you’re fifty dollars out.

    Then there’s deciding. One friend is counting every penny; another is YOLOing through college on daddy’s card. One takes CapMetro because it’s free with a UT ID; another calls an Uber Black. One has a car and will drive anywhere; another thinks anything over twenty minutes is a road trip. Same group chat, completely different Saturdays. So someone asks, “What are we doing?” and forty minutes later, we’re still asking.

    Same group chat, completely different Saturdays.

    So I fixed both. I gave Python my favorite Austin spots, what they actually cost, and, naturally, my opinions. Lattes are for mornings. Brunch comes with mimosas. After Pilates, one showers before being seen in public. Tell it your hours, your mood, your budget and how you’re getting around, and it hands you a day that makes sense, down to the dollar.

    Every Saturday it plans is one I’d actually spend. The spots are the ones I keep going back to. The rules are the ones I live by. The algorithm is the part where I made Python agree with me.

    xoxo,

    ST
