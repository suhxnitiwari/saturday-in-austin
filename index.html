Saturday in Austin

Tell it when you wake up, when you go to bed and how long you want to be out. It plans the day from my favorite Austin spots: coffee first, brunch before noon, dinner somewhere wonderful. Every click is a different Saturday.

python -m saturday

Pick your times and hit plan. The Python code in this repo runs right here in your browser, and every click is a different Saturday.
